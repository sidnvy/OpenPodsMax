# Artwork sources

## Launcher icon

The blue/lavender launcher illustration is the AI-assisted design selected by the project owner. It remains the application icon; the product photographs below are used inside the app and by its optional system-artwork provider.

## AirPods Max product and control images

Version 0.2.2 restores the eight static PNGs used in the earlier private test build, at the project owner's request. They were prepared from Apple's product/specification and support illustrations:

- Product and five finishes: https://www.apple.com/airpods-max/ and https://www.apple.com/airpods-max/specs/
- Control illustrations: https://support.apple.com/guide/airpods/change-settings-for-airpodsmax-dev3e3e8821d/web

Files are `airpods_max_hero.png`, `airpods_max_{midnight,starlight,blue,purple,orange}.png`, and `airpods_crown_detail{,_dark}.png` under `app/src/main/res/drawable-nodpi/`. They are copied byte-for-byte from the owner-selected earlier test assets. No phone identity, logs or signing material is included. The public-file check pins their exact hashes.

Product images remain Apple material and are not covered by the project's GPL grant. Attribution does not represent an Apple license, endorsement or affiliation. The original USDZ and animation derived from it are not included in this static-image release.
