# OpenPodsMax 0.2.12

**Android 17 playback lifecycle fix** — wear-triggered pause/resume now uses
Android's **transient Audio Focus**, avoiding cross-app MediaSession commands
that could leave another player's background audio muted.

## Changes

- When **Pause when removed** and **Resume when worn** are both enabled,
  request temporary Audio Focus after confirmed AirPods removal and release
  it after stable re-wear. The media app owns its foreground service and
  chooses whether to resume.
- Preserve exact selected headset, AAP generation, trusted Bluetooth bond,
  audio route and media-mode checks; release held focus after session reset,
  handoff, or service stop.
- Remove the previous MediaSession-targeted resume controller and its
  notification-listener access requirement. Existing playback preference
  settings survive same-key in-place upgrades.
- Keep the existing pause-only mode for users who disable auto-resume.
  Preserve optional SBC automation, native AirPods handoff and rooted
  Bluetooth identity from 0.2.11.

## Compatibility and limits

- Automatic resume is **application-dependent**: some players do not
  resume on Audio Focus gain, and regular YouTube's background playback
  is subject to its own content/subscription restrictions.
- The official APK does not alter Android's Audio Hardening flags and does
  not require root, accessibility event injection or notification access
  for the new auto-resume path.
- Long-duration reliability, all third-party media apps, calls, other
  headsets and untested Android versions are **not fully validated**.
  The experimental root-identity and device-switching limitations of
  0.2.11 still apply; read [compatibility notes](../COMPATIBILITY.md).
